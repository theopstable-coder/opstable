Is it for you?

You'll fit if you

  • Run or lead operations: last mile, hubs, warehouses, fleets, dark stores, fulfilment or supply chain
  • Are a manager, head, director or above
  • Want straight answers from peers, not vendor decks

Not the right table if you

  • Want to sell services or software to members
  • Are recruiting for an agency
  • Work outside operations

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Founding membership is free. Some in-person events may carry a ticket later.

Who runs The Ops Table?

Team Ops Table, a group of operators. We keep the spotlight on members, not on ourselves.

How are members chosen?

Every application is reviewed for seniority and an operations background. We reply within about a week.

Will my company know what I share?

No. The Chatham House rule applies to every conversation, online and in person.

Can my company sponsor?

Sponsorships will open with our first city meetups. Write to us and we'll keep you posted.

Take a seat at the table.

Applications take about two minutes. Founding members help shape the chapters, the formats and the first meetups.
